Description

The Age Calculator determines the age of a person, or the duration between two dates, in terms of years, months, and days. Simply enter a date of birth and, optionally, a specific date to calculate the age as of. If no 'as of' date is chosen, it will calculate the age based on today's date.

How to Use

  1. Enter Date of Birth: Use the date picker to select the day, month, and year of birth.
  2. Enter 'As of' Date (Optional): If you want to calculate the age at a specific point in time other than today, select that date using the second date picker. If you leave this blank, the age will be calculated based on the current date.
  3. Click "Calculate Age": The result will show the age in years, months, and days.
