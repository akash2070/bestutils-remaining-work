How to Use

This calculator offers three main functions:

  1. What is X% of Y?:
    • Enter the percentage you want to find in the "Percentage (X)" field.
    • Enter the total amount in the "Total Value (Y)" field.
    • Click "Calculate". The result is X% of Y.
  2. X is what % of Y?:
    • Enter the part amount in the "Value (X)" field.
    • Enter the total amount in the "Total Value (Y)" field.
    • Click "Calculate". The result is the percentage that X represents of Y.
  3. Percentage Change (Increase/Decrease):
    • Enter the starting value in the "Initial Value" field.
    • Enter the final value in the "Final Value" field.
    • Click "Calculate". The result is the percentage change (positive for increase, negative for decrease).

Ensure you enter valid numbers in all fields for accurate results.
