How to Use

For each converter (Temperature, Weight, Length):

  1. Enter the numerical value you wish to convert in the "Value" field.
  2. Select the unit you are converting from in the "From" dropdown.
  3. Select the unit you wish to convert to in the "To" dropdown.
  4. Click the "Convert" button. The result will appear below the button.

Please ensure you enter valid numbers for accurate conversions.
