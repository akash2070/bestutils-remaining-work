What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight that applies to adult men and women. It is widely used as a general indicator of whether a person has a healthy body weight for their height.

While BMI can be a useful screening tool, it does not diagnose body fatness or health of an individual. Other factors like muscle mass, bone density, and overall body composition can affect BMI interpretation. Always consult with a healthcare provider for a comprehensive health assessment.

How to Use

  1. Select Units: Choose between Metric (kilograms and centimeters) or Imperial (pounds, feet, and inches) units. The input fields will adjust accordingly.
  2. Enter Weight: Input your current weight in the selected unit.
  3. Enter Height:
    • If using Metric, enter your height in centimeters.
    • If using Imperial, enter your height in feet and inches.
  4. Click "Calculate BMI": The calculator will display your BMI value and the corresponding BMI category.
