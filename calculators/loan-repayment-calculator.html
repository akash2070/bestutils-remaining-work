Description

This calculator helps you estimate the monthly payments for a simple fixed-rate loan, such as a personal loan or a car loan. It uses the standard amortization formula. Please note that this calculation does not include other potential costs like taxes, insurance (for mortgages or car loans), or any other fees associated with the loan. It provides an estimate for the principal and interest components of your payment.

How to Use

  1. Enter Loan Amount: Input the total amount of money you are borrowing (principal).
  2. Enter Annual Interest Rate: Provide the yearly interest rate for the loan (e.g., if the rate is 5%, enter 5).
  3. Enter Loan Term: Specify the duration of the loan in years (e.g., for a 5-year loan, enter 5).
  4. Click "Calculate Monthly Payment": The calculator will show your estimated monthly payment, the total amount you'll repay over the loan term, and the total interest paid.
